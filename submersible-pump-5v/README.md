# Mini Submersible Water Pump 5V 120L/H with 1.5m Cable for Arduino, Raspberry Pi & ESP32

A compact 5V submersible water pump (120 L/H) with a 1.5 m cable for small irrigation, hydroponics, or water circulation projects. The examples show how to drive the pump from an Arduino or Raspberry Pi (via a MOSFET or relay) and how to use a soil moisture sensor to trigger timed watering.
