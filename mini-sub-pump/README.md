# Mini Submersible Water Pump 3-5V for Arduino, Raspberry Pi & ESP32

A 3–5V mini submersible water pump for small microcontroller projects. Use it to build automatic plant watering, desktop fountains, or fluid circulation systems; always drive the pump through a transistor/MOSFET or a relay and provide an appropriate power supply.
